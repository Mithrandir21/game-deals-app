Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Deals, bundles, giveaways and game pages all work without signing in. Signing in with a free IsThereAnyDeal account is optional. It keeps your waitlist, collection and notes in sync across devices, and turns on sale alerts.

Why am I not getting sale alerts?

Alerts need you to be signed in with IsThereAnyDeal and notifications to be allowed for the app. On iPhone, Background App Refresh must also be on (Settings › General › Background App Refresh). The phone decides when the app gets to check in the background, so an alert can arrive a while after a sale starts.

The price in the app differs from the store

Prices come from IsThereAnyDeal and can change at any moment. Make sure your region (Account › Region) matches the store you buy from, and always check the final price at the store before buying.

A game page shows the wrong game

Game details are matched by title, and a few titles are ambiguous. Email us the game's name and we'll look into it.

Why don't I see adult titles?

Games tagged as adult content are hidden by default. You can show them with Account › Show adult titles.

How do I delete my data?

The app keeps its data on your phone; deleting the app removes it. Your IsThereAnyDeal account, waitlist and collection are managed on isthereanydeal.com. For the anonymous crash reports the app sends, see the privacy policy.
